Getting scans to your PC

Tap Share to PC / apps. Your phone's share sheet opens. Pick one of:
• Quick Share / Nearby Share (Android ↔ Windows PC with the app)
• Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox: then open it on your PC
• WhatsApp / Telegram (Web or Desktop) or Email to yourself
If the share sheet isn't available, files are downloaded instead, and you can copy them over with USB or cloud sync.

Camera not opening?

Live camera needs a secure page (https:// or localhost). If you opened this file directly from storage, the live view may be blocked. Use "Phone camera app" in the scan screen instead, which always works. To get live view, host the file on any HTTPS site (GitHub Pages, Netlify Drop, etc.).

Tips for sharp text

• Good light, no shadow of your hand or phone
• Place the page on a dark, plain surface
• Keep the phone parallel to the page
• Use the Scan B&W filter for clean black text on white
• Nothing is uploaded anywhere, all processing happens on your device. Scans are lost if you close the page, so share/download first.
